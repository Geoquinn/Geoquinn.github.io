Teaching approach

Connecting political questions to research practice

My teaching emphasizes clear argument, careful evidence, and the ability to evaluate political claims with appropriate quantitative and qualitative tools.

790:104

Instructor of Record
Summer 2025 & 2026

Introduction to American Government

This course introduces the institutions, actors, and processes that structure American politics. Students examine constitutional design, federalism, Congress, the presidency, courts, parties, elections, public opinion, media, and civil rights.

The course connects institutional rules to contemporary political behavior and asks students to assess competing explanations with evidence. Assignments emphasize analytic reading, concise writing, and the practical interpretation of political data.

Fall 2024 & Spring 2025

Introduction to Political Science Research Methods

This methods course introduces research design, measurement, causal inference, probability, sampling, hypothesis testing, regression, and the interpretation of quantitative evidence.

As a teaching assistant, I supported students as they moved from substantive political questions to testable claims, worked through statistical concepts, and developed reproducible analyses.

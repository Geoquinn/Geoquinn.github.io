About

Political behavior, public opinion, and representation.

George D. Quinn is a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science at Rutgers University, specializing in American politics and statistical methodology.

His dissertation, Negativity Bias Revisited: Changes in the Effects of Campaign Appeals over Time, examines how political communication and campaign environments influence candidate evaluation. His wider research connects survey experiments, open-ended text, electoral returns, demographic data, and political representation.

Quinn is a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Rutgers Department of Political Science and previously served as Lead Graduate Research Assistant for the Young Elected Leaders Project at the Eagleton Institute of Politics. His research has received the 2024 AAPOR Best Student Poster Award.

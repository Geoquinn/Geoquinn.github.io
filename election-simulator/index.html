03 / Methodology

Forecasts should be
auditable.

Every published model should separate observed returns from researcher assumptions, reveal its sensitivity to turnout, and identify where aggregate data cannot support individual-level claims.

01

Start with returns

Certified election results establish the county or district baseline.

02

Model the electorate

Turnout, demographic change, and candidate-specific effects are exposed as assumptions.

03

Stress-test the map

Scenarios show where a result is robust—and where it depends on unusually large shifts.
